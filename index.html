Patient (all runs)
Model parameters for each run
V1
Central volume: the blood and well-perfused organs the drug mixes into first. It sets the size of the bolus needed to reach a plasma target.
V2
Fast peripheral volume: tissues such as muscle that take up drug over minutes.
V3
Slow peripheral volume: mainly fat, which fills over hours. A large V3 means more accumulation in long cases and slower offset.
CL
Elimination clearance: plasma cleared of drug per minute, mostly by the liver. It largely sets the maintenance infusion rate.
Q2, Q3
Intercompartmental clearances: how quickly drug moves between the central volume and the fast (Q2) and slow (Q3) volumes.
k10
Elimination rate constant (CL ÷ V1): the fraction of drug in the central volume removed each minute.
ke0
Effect-site rate constant: how fast brain concentration (Ce) catches up with plasma (Cp). Half-time = 0.693 ÷ ke0, e.g. 0.26/min ≈ 2.7 min.
Ce50
The Ce that gives half the maximum fall in BIS (Eleveld PD model). It falls with age.
Lean mass
The lean-weight measure the model scales with: James LBM for Schnider, Al-Sallami fat-free mass for Eleveld. Marsh uses total weight only.

For teaching and model comparison only, not for clinical dosing. The pump updates every 5 s. Effect-site mode gives the largest rate whose predicted Ce peak stays at or below target, then holds Ce at target. BIS uses the Eleveld PD model (age-adjusted Ce50). Models: Marsh 1991, Schnider 1998/1999, Eleveld 2018.
